The Glitterhame and the Grave Robbers

Session of 17 July 2026

TL;DR

The party descends the Grand Stair into the Glitterhame with Motha's blood-stained bone map. The whispers start at the stair head — "Deliver the message" — and do not stop all session; Eric conceals them from the party throughout. The descent kills six stirges (woken by Eric's armor ringing the black-ore wall) and four grells, one of which paralyzes Eric, crits him for 24, and carries him thirty feet up before he escapes by Passage Mark. The dead duergar outrider at the stair pocket yields 35 gp, a mourning-braid, and a family-crest rubbing — House Moo Goo Gai Pan. In the sepulcher gallery the party identifies the ore itself — Shavat, "Black Khyber," formed where the plane of Shavarath touches the world — finds every casket sealed with an undead inside, and robs the graves: a Belt of Dwarvenkind (worn and attuned by Eric) and five jeweled ceremonial belts (1,250 gp), including goods pulled from the sarcophagus of Queen Pu-Pu Platter Deeptempura, King Corn's mother. The bloom makes repeated contact — Eric fails two Wisdom saves, answers it aloud, sees through its eyes, and is escorted by docile gricks to the courier's body at the heart of the growth: the rejected Ministry requisition, LeBeefe's handwritten instruction to "observe the deep workings and report what listens back," and a burst glass terrarium — the seed of the entire bloom. The bloom's ask, delivered by root and whisper: "Ring it here… when HE is born, it will be too late." Eric advocates ringing the bell, aloud, twice; Gemma physically pulls him away. The Iron Door opens to "shave and a haircut" and says their names. Past the disarmed statue gate, first contact with the kin goes to the edge of violence: Matriarch Madam Jow Teryaki identifies Eric's new belt as her late husband's, names the party grave robbers, and takes it back at weapons' point. Dario salvages the scene in Dwarvish — kinship by marriage through Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat, and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long — and secures one night of the hospitality of Rak'tafur. The returned mourning-braid puts a name on the dead outrider: Sesame Tofu. Madam Jow gives the history of Shavat and the hundreds of bells, and refuses everything else: "You robbed from my dead. You do not have the right." The session closes with Gemma at the council-room door, looking across a removed bridge at the Bladeworks — multiple bells beside the cold lava flow, and an enormous black bell on scaffolding over a train cart. The Thirteenth Casting, found. XP 2,750 each; totals 80,916; level 11 at 85,000.

Beat-by-beat

1. Down the Cold Stair [0:30–1:11]

Setup: Talespire scale corrected (all measurements doubled), and Motha's bone map presented as a handout — drawn in blood, by someone dying, marks legible for the stair, the pocket cave, and the high cavern. At the stair head the whispers begin: "Deliver the message." Eric tells no one.

The descent is lights-out. The rill crossing takes its first toll — Gemma and Dario cross clean; Eric slips (9) and takes Gemma down with him. Eric's History (17) establishes the stairs were built to go up — from the hold below to the door above. His Stealth (double natural 1s) establishes the acoustics of black ore: the wall rings like a drum.

2. The dead mourner and the stirges [1:11–1:31]

The pocket cave holds a grey-skinned dwarf, weeks dead, curled around a stomach wound. On the body: 35 gp, a mourning-braid — white hair wrapped in ribboned Dwarvish prayers for "the souls and the crafts of the Deeptempura people" (Religion 24) — and a wax rubbing of a family crest, christened at the table House Moo Goo Gai Pan and canonized on the spot.

Eric's armor rings the wall; the colony wakes. Six stirges die in under two rounds: Dario's thrown handaxe and sword, Eric's burning hands, Gemma's eldritch blast. The whisper afterward: "He kills us… he kills us." The wet slope then takes 8 bludgeoning each from Eric and Gemma on a failed re-descent.

3. The grells [1:33–2:11]

The whispers ask a question first: "Are you here to kill us or deliver the message?" Gemma's Perception (25) spots the shapes among the stalactites — no surprise. Four grells attack in beak-and-tentacle pairs. The fight's crisis is Eric's: a tentacle paralyzes him (Constitution 10), his spirit guardians drop, a crit takes him for 24, and the grell carries him thirty feet up — "trying to burrow into your body to pull your liver out with its beak" — while the whisper becomes a screech: "He is food. He is not here to deliver the message." Eric breaks the paralysis (Constitution 18) and escapes via the Passage Mark. Gemma kills one from inside its own grapple — "This is how we send a message back where I'm from" — and Bright Dawn and Dario's crits account for the rest. Four grells, six stirges: the table's tally. Investigation (19) after: small eaten skeletons and ruined provisions, the same age as the dead mourner.

4. Rest, and a choice of doors [2:12–2:22]

Short rest on the stairs. Eric's Wisdom save (18) holds the whispers at the door. In-character religion round the fire: Dario, knight of the Covenant of the Flame; Eric, "an orphan that was forced to learn math and worship it"; Gemma names her patron — Kitsune — and Dave issues the standing warning: he has to lie. Route decision: the wet crawl or the masoned path. Eric overrules the hole: "We're not looking for a nest of monsters. We're looking for a foundry." The masoned path leads into the sepulcher structure.

5. The sepulchers, Shavat, and the theft [2:21–3:12]

Everlasting flames, luminescent crystal, and through the archways the first sight of the Glitterhame — and the bloom: "a forest of glowing, even breathing, and — to you — whispering fungus." The spores reach Eric with a clear voice: "Have you come to deliver the message… as my lord sent you?" He answers "Yes" — aloud — and fails the Wisdom save (8): "You suddenly feel less alone inside your skull." The bloom asks to look out through him. He refuses.

Dario's smith's tools name the ore: Shavat — Shavarite, "Black Khyber" — formed wherever the plane of Shavarath, the eternal war, touches the mortal world; forgeable only in infernal heat; his mother called it a myth. His Divine Sense reads the gallery: an undead sealed in every casket, held since the bell rang. The party takes the grave goods anyway: two living wreaths hold six dwarven ceremonial belts; Eric attunes the newest — a Belt of Dwarvenkind — over the table's stated warning. The royal sarcophagus — Queen Pu-Pu Platter Deeptempura, mother of King Corn — resists Athletics, plant growth, and fire, and stays sealed; five jeweled belts (250 gp each) come away regardless. Divine Sense also confirms why nothing grows here: the ground is consecrated, and the bloom stops at the stone's edge.

6. Across the Glitterhame, down the falls [3:15–3:58]

On the cavern floor the bloom makes its second approach — Eric saves (22) and refuses "administrator access." The party finds the kin's trail instead: a rope line, a cold camp, mushroom "fish" drying on cords. A masoned stair down beside the falls delivers them early to the level below: a pool of blind white fish, two swollen doors, and a dozen skeletons in the silt, limbs bound in irons. Four rise; Gemma blasts two back into the water, Lalo Kitsune — her summoned fey fox, fresh from the armor's charges — drops a darkness cube, and Dario finishes the rest. The chains are Imperial standard-issue slave shackles, three years rusted. Beyond: a bare room scarred by old fighting; a cistern whose iron hatch opens on "a writhing pile of bones and skulls… so many packed into such a small space they can barely constitute themselves" — slammed shut; and low on one wall, at a child's height, a chalk drawing of a bell and stick-figure dwarves with their hands over their ears.

7. The pudding and the stores [3:58–4:23]

In the rotted storeroom Gemma's Perception (24) finds the black substance curled around the center beam before it drops. Eric's faerie fire outlines it; Dario's handaxe splits it in two; Eric's 2nd-level guiding bolt detonates one half — scored, at the table, to the Oppenheimer soundtrack — and Bright Dawn takes the other. The stores are Imperial-sealed: mining and smithing hardware, stationery, medical supplies under Black Dove heraldry, and sealed lamp oil — Gemma takes fifty flasks into the bag of holding. Eric's attunement completes on the walk back: beard stubble, +2 Constitution, darkvision, poison resistance.

8. The courier, and the bloom's pitch [4:28–4:54]

The whispers acquire a title: "Come to me. I have the message. The Lord Mayor wills it." Eric eats a mushroom, fails his second Wisdom save — "you suddenly feel like someone is looking out from your eyes" — and through the shared sight spots two gricks hidden in the rockfall, which regard him and stand down. The bloom walks the party to the center of the growth: a skeleton in professional clothes, the mayoral brooch of Newham, the largest fungal tree rooted through the neck of his coat. The satchel holds the rejected requisition — a Ministry of Transportation stoppage order over the signature of the acting Secretary, John C. LeBeefe — his handwritten instruction to "observe the deep workings and report what listens back," which Eric recognizes on sight, and a burst glass terrarium: shattered from the inside by growth. The seed of everything on this level.

Then the pitch, root on Eric's shoulder: "You are his little window. Our Lord looks out of you and does not know it… Ring it here. It will bring her plan to ruin. Wait for her to do it — when HE is born, it will be too late." Eric, aloud, to the party: "Dario, Gemma, I really think we should consider ringing that bell." Gemma: "Ringing the bell is exactly what we're trying to stop." The forest ripples as she pulls him out of it; the bloom offers to consume his friends. He declines that too.

9. Shave and a haircut [4:54–5:29]

The Iron Door: smith-marked, riveted, no keyhole, KNOCK AND BE KNOWN. Eric knocks "shave and a haircut." The door opens — and in its creak it says their names: "Eric. Dario. Gemma." Beyond, the statue gate: chalk arrows on the blue tile, both pressure plates already jammed with someone's thieves' tools, both iron-bound doors false. Dario's thrown warhammer proves the axe trap still works; Gemma re-jams the plate (25, after one near-miss under the swing). Behind the shield statue, a leather scrap carries the knock written in Dwarvish — "Shave. And a haircut." — which Eric had already guessed. The knock opens a floor passage under the wall.

10. Madam Jow Teryaki [5:30–6:01]

The Great Hall: ten columns carved as giants and drakes, a throne on a dais, and a camp — a shielded truce-lamp, fourteen bedrolls, a field-shrine of recovered tokens, an old dwarf writing names onto a roll. The matriarch: "I am Madam Jow Teryaki. This is my family's home. Who are you?"

The party is dressed as Imperial spies, and the audience opens on that footing. Gemma dismisses the seeming. Dario saves it in Dwarvish: greetings from the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long, kinship by marriage — son-in-law of Yellow Meat, husband of Orange Chicken — verified and accepted. Then the kin's caster points at Eric's waist. The Belt of Dwarvenkind is Madam Jow's late husband's. "You trespass on my family's hold. You desecrate our honored dead. And you steal my late husband's belt." Weapons out. Eric hands it over; it is locked in a chest — "You might get it back. We'll see." Gemma's cover story (Deception 13) is disbelieved on its face.

Dario formally begs the hospitality of Rak'tafur and receives one cold night of it: "Do not overstay your welcome, grave robbers." Gemma returns the mourning-braid, and the dead outrider gets his name: "It's Sesame Tofu's. Thank you for returning this… you can sleep in Sesame Tofu's bunk. You won't be needing it." Madam Jow then gives the fullest account of the mountain anyone has: the elves of Aerenal who came to marvel and stayed to enslave; Shavat hammered thin as paper, layered, encoding "millions of imperceptible shapes and circuits of golden wire," casting "vast and devastating spells across distances, like sound"; the retaken mine set to making a bell, then several, then hundreds; tens of thousands of kin slaughtered and left in mass graves; the clan returned "to consecrate the dead and to take this place back over." And she closes the door on the rest: "I will not let go of the secrets of the dwarves so that you can replicate her horrors. You robbed from my dead. You do not have the right."

11. The Thirteenth Casting [6:01–6:19]

At camp, the party quietly debates stealing the belt back, and shelves it — "It's a fair cop. They were right." Gemma explores: behind the throne, an unlocked iron door into the council room — a table of black Khyber cut from a single slab, a stone door carved as a dwarf's face, a young sentry ("Unplug your hole"). And past the sentry's don't: sulfur and heat, the lava flows still glowing years after the works went dark, a removed bridge — and across the gap, the Bladeworks. Multiple bells sit inverted by the melt. Left of them, on scaffolding, over a train cart, an enormous black bell, the collapsed rail tunnel behind it.

Eric steps out to look. The whisper, one more time: "Deliver the message." End of session.

Intel & open threads

  • The ore is named: Shavat ("Black Khyber," Shavarite) — planar ore of Shavarath, infernal-heat forging only, and the medium of the bells: paper-thin layers encoding circuit-work that casts spells at range "like sound." Madam Jow's history confirms hundreds of bells, not thirteen — the thirteen Masters sit atop a much larger production run.
  • Consecration happens at delivery, not at the foundry (per the manifest, confirmed at the table): the bell across the foundry floor "is just a bell." The party's own synthesis at wrap-up: a counter-casting is conceivable — Gemma's proposal, a silence-scroll infused casting, "a bell that makes silence on a large scale."
  • The bloom's agenda, stated plainly: it wants the bell rung now, claims to speak for "the Lord Mayor" (the LeBeefe courier's title), and warns "when HE is born, it will be too late" — undecoded, presumed to point at Uriel's pursuit of a son. Nichole's table-theory, partially confirmed by the DM: LeBeefe seeded the foundry so any rung bell raises infected dead outside Uriel's control.
  • Eric's compromise deepened: two failed Wisdom saves (spore contact; the eaten mushroom), the bloom sees through his eyes at will when he fails, the gricks treat him as kin, and he has now advocated ringing the bell aloud — while concealing the whispers from the party entirely. Gemma and Dario know only that "you've been acting very strange since we've been here, and you just ate a mushroom."
  • Kin standing: one night of Rak'tafur hospitality, bought by Dario's marriage-kinship and the returned braid; poisoned by the discovered grave-robbery. The Belt of Dwarvenkind is surrendered and locked in Madam Jow's chest ("you might get it back"). The five jeweled belts (1,250 gp) are still in the party's bags, and the kin said "grave robbers" to their faces. All bell-lore cooperation refused.
  • The royal sarcophagus stayed sealed — Queen Pu-Pu Platter Deeptempura, held shut by "some unseen force" beyond its weight; an undead is sealed in every casket in the gallery, and the mass-grave cistern below barely holds its dead. The sepulcher ground is consecrated — the one place the bloom cannot enter.
  • Unclaimed mysteries: who disarmed the statue-gate plates and left their thieves' tools; how deep the "door that says their names" knowledge goes; whether the sealed dead can be laid to rest; where the Original bell is ("probably where Uriel is").
  • Loot: 35 gp; the wax crest rubbing; 5 jeweled belts (1,250 gp, unreturned); 50 flasks of sealed lamp oil; the requisition, LeBeefe's letter, and the terrarium. The mourning-braid returned. The Belt of Dwarvenkind worn for half a session and confiscated; Eric's attunement slot open, the pearl of power back in the pool.
  • XP: 2,750 each — running totals 80,916. Level 11 at 85,000 (4,084 out).
