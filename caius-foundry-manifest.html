KORRANBERG FOUNDRY TRUST

Private Schedule of Works — undertaken for the House of Caius, by standing draft.
Second leaf. Continued from the first.

STANDING SUMMARY, CARRIED FORWARD

— Great castings, consecration grade: THIRTEEN commissioned. ELEVEN delivered. TWO in the yard.

— Minor castings, parish grade: three hundred forty-one delivered to the Houses to date; schedule of small consignments kept separately by the Sisters and not by this office.

— All castings consecrated on delivery, by the Sisters alone. Foundry personnel are excluded from consecration and are reminded that curiosity is a breach of the draft.

CONSIGNMENTS, CONTINUED

¶ 09. Great casting the ninth. Consigned to the cathedral-house at KORTH. Delivered; consecrated; receipt under the dove seal.

¶ 10. Great casting the tenth. Consigned to the monastery precincts at ATUR. Delivered standing; the tower to be raised over it at the consignee's expense. Receipt under the dove seal.

¶ 11. Great casting the eleventh. Consigned to the Grand Infirmary at FAIRHAVEN. Delivered by twenty-ox dray, Sister riding escort. Receipt under the dove seal.

¶ 12. Great casting the twelfth. Consigned to — freight instructions withheld from this office; wagons provided sealed; drivers of the Sisters' own. Draft paid double for the blindness.

¶ 13. Great casting the thirteenth. In the yard. Tuning not yet taken. See memorandum below.

¶ ∅. The Original. Not of this foundry, and the Trust will thank the House to record that it never was. Received under guard for the taking of tuning references only — our masters worked in wax and never with hammers, per the terms. Returned under escort to Her Radiance's keeping; destination withheld from schedule; escort Sister-Inquisitor M. D., dove seal. The Trust petitions that no further reference work be sent to this yard. The men will not stay in the shop with it after dark, and the Trust has stopped fining them for it.

Memorandum on the thirteenth: casting the thirteenth cannot take its tuning until the reference is consigned to us again, and the House's factor writes that the reference will not travel again before the appointed night. The thirteenth therefore waits on Her Radiance's convenience. The Trust notes for the record that it has met its dates and that the choir wants only what the House itself withholds.

Drafts current. Dockets burned per standing instruction, this leaf excepted in error.
