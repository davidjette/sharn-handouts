TL;DR

A rest day that refused to stay restful. The party holds the Door to let Dario heal: Eric cobbles Gemma's shoes, Gemma reveals she can scribe fireball scrolls and starts one, and Braga Kul asks for a dagger to kill himself rather than be taken by "Imperial troops and Black Doves." Eric lays the five-card tarot augury on the descent — Tower, Moon, Devil, Hanged Man, Judgement: weal AND woe — and hears the whispers again while examining the unnamed skull, which Gemma's nat-20 pegs as no natural creature, kin to the one-eyed floaters they fought outside Leaf years ago. On Eric's watch, tapping: far-off steel from above answered by Braga rapping code on his bars with what turns out to be a sending stone. The truth-serum-by-mage-hand gambit fails on Braga's CON save, parley fails on his defiance, and then the front door opens — the returning scout party, eight strong, is home. Gemma magic-missiles Braga unconscious mid-fight (he does not survive it); Eric's wall of fire seals the door, burns the rope bridge to nothing, and cooks half the relief force; spirit guardians (the golden math symbols) eat most of the rest; an orc warlock suicide-bombs the barracks with black powder crying "I'd die for the people." One wounded orc found outside is bagged as a replacement prisoner — then forgotten until morning, when Gemma quietly cuts his throat before the descent. Every body burned, into the crevasse. Braga's roster math confirms it: the entire ~20-strong Urukoth recon regiment is destroyed — and his records show no mention of the bell. Morning: Dario's up (diamond glinting in the empty socket), disguises back on, and Gemma spends six deafening minutes of eldritch blast breaking the dwarven gate to the stair room — waking a stirge swarm (dispatched) and unveiling the relief carvings: King Corn of the Deeptempura, the Glitterhame named in stone, an infernal pact with an unnamed devil to forge the black ore, and a peace treaty with a dragon — Vuldrach, whom Dario's history knows as an elder black dragon of the north. Kitsune's old warning suddenly relevant: "Never negotiate with an insane dragon." XP to 78,166 each (level 11 at 85,000). Paused fully rested at the top of the stair.

Beat-by-beat

1. Holding the Door [0:00–0:13]

Housekeeping from Friday: the party has found the blocked stair area behind the crates, and Braga's notes confirm his men fought gricks below and came back "retching and talking about whispers." Shown the map, Braga is frank from the cell:

Braga Kul: "You'd be a fool to unblock it. I'm safe in here — unless you intend to unlock this door."

Dario needs another full rest, and the debate (go down now vs. wait) lands on waiting: nobody wants Dario waking alone next to the orc who killed him. Braga asks for a dagger — for himself.

Braga Kul: "Do you think I'm going to allow myself to be taken alive by Imperial troops and Black Doves?"

2. Crafting day [0:13–0:36]

Gemma bars the great door and locks down the complex. Eric spends eight hours with his father's cobbling kit modifying Gemma's boots (advantage on Acrobatics — it pays off within hours). Gemma reveals two sleeper capabilities: a truth serum she brewed on her three-year quest, and — to the table's delight — scroll scribing (calligrapher's tools + Arcana): "I can give you a scroll of fireball." Rules settled: 500 gp and a 40-hour workweek, gold melted into the ink; she starts one. The Sharn apartment's home-defense crystal gets set at the door; the password, from the old days, is "LeBeefe" — and when Eric speaks it aloud, he hears his name in the whispers again.

3. The skull and the spread [0:16–0:29]

The unnamed skull off Braga's trophy door: Gemma's natural 20 Nature says this is no type of natural creature — and it strongly resembles the one-eyed, tentacled things that floated out of the morning mist near Leaf years ago (Jeff's year-later interlude). While she examines it, Eric hears the whispers. Unresolved, deliberately.

Then Eric shuffles the dead fortune-teller's tarot and casts augury on the descent — five cards, laid like stairs:

Where you stand: THE TOWER — the fortress, and the disaster that already happened here. What waits below: THE MOON — a false light; things are not what they seem. The voice you've been hearing: THE DEVIL — a bargainer, dangerous to trust, himself in chains. The sense that this place has infernal influence. The price: THE HANGED MAN. Your salvation: JUDGEMENT — angels blowing horns over open graves.

The spell's answer: weal and woe. Eric connects the dots out loud: the bells are infernal metal, and the dwarves "dug too deep" — this is where it comes from.

4. Tapping in the dark [0:36–0:58]

On Eric's watch, ~midnight: faint tapping on steel, far away, above — answered by a clear rhythm from down the hall. Braga, head down, rapping a rock against his bars. Eric's stealth peek (21): the war-chief is sending and receiving. One loud final tap, and Braga hides the rock and leans back. Eric wakes Gemma ("say the LeBeefe") and they move on the cell — first with the truth serum in a "water" delivery via invisible mage hand while Braga sleeps. He wakes, swipes, misses — it goes in his mouth — he spits, but enough lands: DC 11 CON save… which he makes on a 16. Parley (Gemma masked, as "Red") goes nowhere:

Braga Kul: "Let me out and I'll tell you… You should get out of here right now. That's my advice to you." Braga Kul: "My reason is that it's your only chance to avoid what's about to happen."

Perception 22: the great door — the one Gemma barred — opens.

5. The scouts come home [1:06–2:41]

The Uruk foraging/scout party (the module's wilderness pressure, eight strong with a warlock and a masked bone-white scout) is home, signaled in by their war-chief. Initiative. The fight runs the whole length of the Door and back:

  • Gemma opens by putting three magic missiles through the cell bars into Braga — he goes down, and a stone drops from his hand ("probably a sending stone").
  • Eldritch blast knocks one orc off the broken bridge (crit-failed catch, into the rift); another gets blasted 20 feet back out the front door into her squadmates.
  • Eric's wall of fire (4th slot) seals the doorway — 60 feet of it. The rope bridge catches and burns away entirely ("a burnt bridge is a lot harder to fix"). Two orcs die running through it; the screaming is constant.
  • The survivors flood the secret gallery passage toward the barracks — exactly the route the party found first. Eric drops the wall for spirit guardians (the golden math symbols), which kill two on arrival and three more across the fight ("like being attacked by piranhas… the holy symbols just rip them to pieces").
  • A crit arrow takes Eric in the head (19 piercing, "arrow sticking out of your head like Steve Martin"); the Warden Statue's coin-blessing — poison immunity, bought with an offering to the founder's statue after it gassed him — has expired, a fact he learns the hard way. "Our guardian angel perhaps."
  • Gemma acrobatics-flips over a crate (the new shoes) to blade-block a fleeing conscript, then true-strikes her dagger into the base of a skull. Her killing night also includes a nat-20 decapitation and a running debate with the rules on Ready-action multiattacks (settled: one attack).
  • The orc warlock, cornered and burned, says something in Orcish, then in Common — "I'd die for the people" — and smashes a black-powder charge against himself, torching the dormitory.

When it's over: Braga Kul is dead — the magic missiles finished what the cell started. ("He's dead." "Good. Fuck him.") Dario sleeps through all of it.

6. Sweep, and a second prisoner [2:41–3:10]

Gemma repeats the ritual: faces burned, bodies into the crevasse — Braga included, after Eric declines the corpse ("He wasn't very forthcoming in life; I doubt he'll be any more forthcoming in death"). Loot: ~80 gp in jewelry and wages, a priest's pack off the warlock, and a charm meant to be eaten — rotted meat wrapped in a mouse's tail, in a bag sized for several, one left. Eric's 31 Arcana/Religion: primitive, consumable, real. Unidentified; kept.

Outside on the porch sweep: the scouts' last 2–3 days of rations, no horses (Millie Bobby Brown and Bon Jovi, gone — presumed eaten), and one wounded orc asleep against the stone, carted home hurt from wherever the patrol had been. Gemma walks out of the darkness in the fox mask, layers sleep on top of his sleep, bags his head, and locks him in Braga's cell. Braga's campaign records, re-read against the roster: every name is now accounted for — killed below by the gricks, or killed today. The regiment is gone. And decisive intel: nothing in their records mentions the bell — troop movements in and out of Korth, intercepted communications, stay hidden. The orcs were never here for what the party is here for.

7. Morning, and a quiet murder [3:10–3:20]

Dario wakes whole — exhaustion gone, one eye short, "in the center of the scab, this disconcerting sparkle." Breakfast by the farmer's son from the orc kitchen. Disguises back on (seeming — Imperial costumes). Mission alignment talk over food: find the foundry, learn how the castings were made and whether they can be wounded or silenced, feed "the update" to pre-positioned Silver Flame units at the bell sites. An old divination resurfaces and hangs over everything below:

Kitsune (contact other planes, recalled): "Never negotiate with an insane dragon."

Then the second prisoner, forgotten all morning, is remembered at the top of the stair. He can barely hold himself up. Gemma takes him to the edge where the bridge was — then, without Eric or Dario seeing, cuts his throat, burns him, and puts him over the side. No bodies left behind.

8. Six minutes of noise [3:20–3:26]

The stair-room gate: dwarven iron, something snapped off in the lock, effectively barred. Dario can't rip it down even with guidance. So Gemma settles in with eldritch blast — AC 20, and the DM runs the math live ("hold on a second, running Python scripts") — six minutes of continuous blasting, tremendous ringing noise through the whole complex, before the original dwarven gate cracks and falls. The noise bill comes due immediately: a stirge swarm in the upper dark of the stair room, thoroughly awake (retconned in honestly: "you just banged that thing for six minutes; those things have been going crazy"), is blasted apart. +200 XP each, session-lateness discount.

9. The stair room: King Corn, the pact, and the treaty [3:26–3:47]

Beyond the gate: scattered dwarvish dead, three-to-four years old, in rags — massacre victims who made it this far up. And on the walls, masterwork relief carvings flanking the descending stair. Eric (Religion 32, reading the Dwarvish): the left wall is the record of an infernal pact — a crowd of dwarves led by a king with seven stars on his crown (the same figure as the Warden Statue) walking down into a mountain of polished black ore, and at the wall's end, the king looking up at a huge winged, horned devil pointing at an anvil. The text names names: King Corn of the Deeptempura; the discovery of the GLITTERHAME, a cavern full of infernal ore; a deal with an unnamed devil to learn how to forge it. The right wall: dwarves at anvils holding swords and axes, ending in a drake wrapped around an anvil, the king before it with arms outstretched — a peace treaty with a dragon named VULDRACH. Dario's History (19): Vuldrach is a name he knows — an elder black dragon, known to live somewhere in the north. Black dragons are secretive; nobody knows more.

The party assembles it at the table: the ore is here, the forge is likely here, the clan's treasure would be here — fiends, a dragon, and everything the bells are made of, under their feet. The augury said weal and woe; Kitsune said never negotiate with an insane dragon; and the Teryaki mourners are somewhere ahead of them in the dark, possibly traveling under land.

Paused: fully long-rested, at the top of the cleared staircase, about to descend. Next session picks up on the other side.
