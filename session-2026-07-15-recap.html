TL;DR

The party holds the Door for a rest day so Dario can recover. Eric cobbles Gemma's boots; Gemma reveals she can scribe spell scrolls and starts a fireball scroll; Braga Kul, interrogated, asks for a dagger to kill himself rather than be taken alive. Eric lays a five-card tarot augury on the descent — Tower, Moon, Devil, Hanged Man, Judgement — and the spell answers weal and woe. On night watch Eric hears tap-code: signals from above, answered by Braga rapping a rock on his cell bars. The truth-serum attempt fails on Braga's save; parley fails on his refusal; then the barred front door opens from the inside — the warband's scout party, eight strong, is back, and one of them was already in the fortress. Gemma magic-missiles Braga through the bars; he dies with the rock still in his fist, held as a weapon. Eric's wall of fire closes the doorway and burns the rope bridge away entirely; spirit guardians kill most of the rest; the orc war-caster suicide-bombs the barracks with black powder — "I'd die for the people." A wounded straggler found outside is taken prisoner, forgotten until morning, and executed by Gemma before the descent, unwitnessed. Roster math against Braga's records: the entire ~20-strong Urukoth recon regiment is destroyed, and its records contain no mention of the bell — the orcs were watching Korth, nothing more. Loot includes a bag built for several doses with one left: rotted meat wrapped in a mouse's tail — a potion of lesser polymorph (mouse only), which is how the scouts got inside a barred fortress: through the arrow slits. Morning: disguises on, six minutes of eldritch blast breaks the dwarven stair-room gate, a stirge swarm dies for the noise, and the stair reliefs are read — King Corn of the Deeptempura, the Glitterhame named in stone, an infernal pact for the forging of the black ore, and a peace treaty with the dragon Vuldrach, whom Dario knows as an elder black dragon of the north. XP 78,166 each (level 11 at 85,000). Paused at the top of the stair, fully rested, descending.

Beat-by-beat

1. Holding the Door [0:00–0:13]

Carry-over from 7/11 resolved: the party has found the blocked stair area behind the crates. Braga's notes confirm his men fought gricks below and came back "retching and talking about whispers." Shown the map, he answers from the cell:

Braga Kul: "You'd be a fool to unblock it. I'm safe in here — unless you intend to unlock this door."

Dario needs another full rest. The party decides to wait rather than leave him alone with the orc who killed him. Braga requests a dagger — for himself:

Braga Kul: "Do you think I'm going to allow myself to be taken alive by Imperial troops and Black Doves?"

2. Crafting day [0:13–0:36]

Gemma bars the great door and locks down the complex. Eric spends eight hours with his father's cobbling kit on Gemma's boots (advantage on Acrobatics; used in combat the same night). Gemma discloses two capabilities: a truth serum brewed on her three-year quest, and scroll scribing (calligrapher's tools + Arcana) — "I can give you a scroll of fireball." Rules settled at 500 gp and a 40-hour workweek, gold melted into the ink; she starts one. The Sharn apartment's home-defense crystal is set at the camp door. Its password is "LeBeefe" — and when Eric speaks it, he hears his name in the whispers.

3. The skull and the spread [0:16–0:29]

The unnamed skull from Braga's trophy door: Gemma's natural-20 Nature check establishes it is no natural creature, and that it resembles the one-eyed, tentacled things the party fought outside Leaf years ago. Eric hears the whispers while she handles it. No further identification; left open.

Eric then casts augury through the dead fortune-teller's tarot deck, on the question of going downstairs. Five cards:

Where you stand: THE TOWER — the fortress, and the disaster that already happened in it. What waits below: THE MOON — false light; things are not what they seem. The voice you've been hearing: THE DEVIL — a bargainer, dangerous to trust, itself in chains. The sense of infernal influence on the place. The price: THE HANGED MAN. Your salvation: JUDGEMENT — angels blowing horns over open graves.

The spell's answer: weal and woe. Eric connects the bell lore aloud: the bells are infernal metal, and this mountain is where such metal comes from.

4. Tap-code [0:36–0:58]

On Eric's watch, around midnight: faint tapping on steel, far off and above — answered by a clear rhythm from down the hall. Braga, head down, is rapping a rock against his cell bars. Eric observes unseen (Stealth 21): exchange, then one loud final tap, and Braga palms the rock and settles back. Eric wakes Gemma. They move on the cell: first the truth serum, delivered into the sleeping orc's mouth by invisible mage hand. Braga wakes mid-pour, swipes, misses — and makes the save (16 vs. DC 11). Parley, Gemma masked as "Red," produces nothing but warnings:

Braga Kul: "Let me out and I'll tell you… You should get out of here right now. That's my advice to you." Braga Kul: "My reason is that it's your only chance to avoid what's about to happen."

Perception 22: the great door — barred from the inside since evening — opens.

5. The scouts return [1:06–2:41]

The warband's scout-and-forage party, eight strong including a war-caster and a masked scout, is home. They had signaled ahead; Braga's taps were the answer; and at least one scout was already inside the fortress to lift the bar (see Intel — the mouse-charm). The fight runs the length of the Door:

  • Gemma opens with three magic missiles through the cell bars. Braga drops. The rock falls from his hand — palmed since the tapping stopped, held as his last weapon.
  • Eldritch blast knocks one orc off the broken bridge into the rift; a second is blasted 20 feet back out the front door.
  • Eric casts wall of fire (4th-level slot) across the doorway. The rope bridge ignites and burns away completely. Two orcs die crossing the wall; the survivors reroute through the gallery passages toward the barracks.
  • Eric drops the wall for spirit guardians, which kill two orcs on arrival and three more over the fight. A crit arrow hits Eric in the head (19 piercing). The Warden Statue's coin-blessing — poison immunity, purchased with an offering after the gas trap — has expired; the poisoned arrows now cost him.
  • Gemma flips over a crate (the new boots, Acrobatics 18) to block a fleeing conscript at swordpoint, then kills her with a true-strike dagger to the base of the skull. She also takes a head with a natural 20 earlier in the fight.
  • The war-caster, cornered and burned, speaks in Orcish, then in Common — "I'd die for the people" — and smashes a black-powder charge against himself. The dormitory catches fire.

Braga Kul is confirmed dead after the fight — the magic missiles finished him. ("He's dead." "Good. Fuck him.") Dario sleeps through the entire engagement.

6. Sweep and a second prisoner [2:41–3:10]

Gemma repeats her protocol: faces burned, bodies into the crevasse, Braga included. Loot: ~80 gp in jewelry and wages, a priest's pack from the war-caster, and a small bag sized for several doses holding one charm meant to be eaten — rotted meat wrapped in a mouse's tail. Eric's Arcana/Religion 31: primitive, consumable, real; effect not determined at the table (see Intel).

The porch sweep finds the scouts' remaining rations (2–3 days), no horses (Millie Bobby Brown and Bon Jovi, gone), and one wounded orc asleep against the stone, carted back injured from the patrol. Gemma approaches in the fox mask, casts sleep on top of his sleep, bags and binds him, and locks him in Braga's cell. Braga's records, checked against the roster: every name accounted for — killed below by the gricks, or killed tonight. The records document troop movements in and out of Korth and intercepted communications. Nothing about the bell.

7. Morning [3:10–3:20]

Dario wakes recovered — exhaustion cleared, one eye gone, the revivify diamond visible in the socket. He cooks breakfast from the orc stores. Disguises are re-applied (seeming; the Imperial costumes). Over food, the party restates the mission: find the foundry, learn how the castings were made and whether they can be broken or silenced, and feed any method to pre-positioned Silver Flame units at the bell sites. An earlier divination is recalled and logged against what's below:

Kitsune (contact other planes, recalled): "Never negotiate with an insane dragon."

The second prisoner is remembered only at the top of the stair. Gemma takes him to the bridge gap and, unwitnessed by Eric or Dario, cuts his throat, burns the body, and drops it over. No bodies left behind.

8. The gate [3:20–3:26]

The stair-room gate: dwarven iron, the key snapped off in the lock, effectively barred. Dario cannot force it, even with guidance. Gemma works it down with eldritch blast — AC 20, roughly six minutes of continuous fire (the DM computes it live) — and the gate cracks off its pintle. The noise carries through the complex for the duration. A stirge swarm in the stair-room dome, agitated by the six minutes of hammering, attacks and is destroyed. +200 XP each.

9. The stair room [3:26–3:47]

Beyond the gate: scattered dwarvish dead, three to four years old, in rags — massacre victims who reached the gate from the inside. Flanking the descending stair, masterwork relief carvings, read by Eric (Religion 32; he speaks Dwarvish):

  • Left wall: a crowd of dwarves led by a king with seven stars on his crown — the same figure as the Warden Statue — descending into a mountain of polished black ore; at the wall's end, the king before a huge winged, horned devil pointing at an anvil. The text names King Corn of the Deeptempura, the discovery of the GLITTERHAME — "a cavern full of infernal ore" — and a bargain with an unnamed devil for the art of forging it. Confirmed as the formal record of an infernal pact.
  • Right wall: dwarves at anvils bearing swords and axes, ending in a drake coiled around an anvil, the king before it with arms open — a peace treaty with the dragon Vuldrach. Dario's History (19): Vuldrach is a known name — an elder black dragon living somewhere in the north. Black dragons are secretive; nothing more is known.

The table's synthesis: the ore is here, the forge is likely here, the clan's treasure would be here — and a dragon is on the ledger. The augury said weal and woe; Kitsune said never negotiate with an insane dragon; the Teryaki mourners are somewhere ahead in the dark, possibly traveling under land.

Paused: fully long-rested, at the top of the cleared staircase, descending. Next session picks up on the other side.
