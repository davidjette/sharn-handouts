TL;DR

Digma Beeve relays Orange Chicken's letter about the lost Deeptempura forge-clan and the Teryaki mourning-party, and the party connects it to the manifest's foundry. Riding out of Korth as Deirdre, Graham, and Sir Beef Taco, they con the Korranberg Trust office out of its records (Eric nat-20 audit): dwarvish slaves owned by the Trust, ~7 years of castings shipped by a rail that officially never existed, and a handwritten tally showing ~97% of the dwarvish workforce destroyed in "the accident," ~3 years ago — the same year Sharn fell. They assault the Mountain Door head-on and clear it in a long, ugly fight: barracks fireballed, the rope bridge half-cut, Eric dangling one-handed over the rift, and Dario executed on the ground by War-Chief Braga Kul — then revivified by Eric with a diamond sliced into his skull, twinning him with the Empress. Dario returns one-eyed, with a death-vision of dragons and white sands. Braga Kul surrenders and is taken captive (un-interrogated); every other orc dies; Gemma burns all the bodies — including the five originals from the bags of holding — so Uriel can never raise them. Loot includes the Deeptempura handaxe and Braga's campaign book: the orcs were a ~20-strong Urukoth recon regiment watching Korth — the war is coming to Korth, and the first troops are already moving. XP 7,700 each (totals 69,866, level 10). The stair down is blocked with rock and dirt; the party suspects the forge itself lies elsewhere in the mountain.

Beat-by-beat

1. Korth, as the Empire's people [0:00–0:16]

The session opens in Deirdre's apartment — inventory of the murdered originals' effects (divining bones, 250 gp; tarot; a short rifle; the makeup mirror). Korth canon on the screen-share tour: the E.D.E.N. induction cathedral turning corpses into soldiers, the boiler plant, the Rekkenmark, the palace staffed for a throne unsat in a decade — and the detail that something wards Korth against aerial attack, which may be a secret of the Emperor's real power.

Digma Beeve reaches Gemma by sending stone: the dwarves answered Dario's bell inquiry at once. Orange Chicken's letter delivers the hook — the Deeptempura clan, the stolen forge under the Korramont, the Teryaki mourners out of the Mror Holds.

DM (Dave): "Saddest thing I ever heard in my own house."

2. Deirdre's paperwork is a skeleton key [0:16–0:52]

Graham audits Deirdre's portfolio: due-diligence law, weights-and-measures inspection rights at every port of entry including the lightning rail — and standing clearance to the summer palace and the Lazaret. The table clocks it immediately. Her live case: an Imperial decree to raise a colonel's rabbits-per-home limit, requiring the comatose Emperor's override.

The rail discovery starts here: her comprehensive station list has no Korranberg line; an older map shows one.

3. The road north [1:05–1:27]

Karrnathi countryside — canals, grain trains, undead laborers rotting in the fields. Eric (17 Perception) spots the old rail cut: rails purposefully removed, not destroyed, freight-gauge, running into the King's Forest. In Korranberg town: the dry fountain, the freight yard brazier and six idle guards, and the widow Marest at the boarding house — paid in gold per question. The rail was "took apart a long time ago when they closed the foundry and the mine." There was "an accident." She will not be tricked into more.

4. The audit con at the Trust office [1:29–1:53]

The clerk — Austin, first job, angling for a five-star rating — accepts the Imperial credentials, unlocks the record crates, and waits outside smoking. Eric's natural 20 (with CPA advantage) produces the session's biggest intel haul:

  • No records at all for the last three years.
  • ~7 years of freight schedules before that: ore in, "large classified objects" out — the manifest's castings. The improved rail was built when the House of Caius bought the mountain ~10–11 years ago.
  • Archaic property records of dwarvish slaves owned by the Trust.
  • A handwritten, unofficial death tally: ~97% of the dwarvish workforce destroyed in "the accident," ~3 years ago — the same year Esther Crona died and Sharn fell. The players theorize on their own that a bell was rung.

Austin: "Of course you understand there is no rail, and there never was a rail."

5. First blood at the porch [2:03–2:17]

The party chooses the front road. Two orc sentries challenge; negotiation is not attempted in good faith; the horn sounds even as Gemma's blast knocks the horn-blower back. Dario mounted-charges up the switchbacks; Eric's Spirit Guardians manifest as golden math symbols — the accountant-cleric's signature all night.

6. The Mountain Door [2:17–2:58]

Archers in the slit galleries feather Dario while the main door stays barred. Gemma misty-steps into an archer nook and clears it at swordpoint with Bright Dawn; Eric takes the opposite gallery and nat-20s the secret door. In the guard rooms: old bones crammed in the corners — years dead, long before the orcs. At the rift hall an orc woman cuts two of the bridge's four ropes before dying; Dario spends the fight locked outside banging on the door until Eric finds the bar.

7. Fire, smoke, and the bridge [3:16–3:58]

Deeper in: a spike-growth caster, a healer the table names "Ork Mama," the archer Orko Arquero. Eric fireballs the barracks — 34 damage, a cluster dead, and every bale of bedding alight; the corridor fills with smoke and the whole fight goes to disadvantage ("the fireball might have been imprudent"). Eric ends up dangling one-handed from the wrecked bridge on a natural 1; the orc who leaps to tackle him misses and goes into the rift alone; Gemma nat-20 climbs down and Dimension Doors them both out.

8. Dario dies [4:27–4:33]

In the camp melee Durz's crushing blow drops Dario — nobody had tracked how hurt he was — and he takes more hits on the ground. Then the war-chief steps over him and cuts his throat.

Braga Kul: "All of his unloved and neglected children will have no father."

9. The counterattack, and a surrender [4:33–5:05]

Gemma spends her last big slot putting herself and Eric into the middle of the camp (after the table rules-debate that ends with Bright Dawn's teleport costing an action). Eric goes nova — 5th-level Spirit Guardians, Radiance of the Dawn three times. Durz loses a leg to a Gemma natural 20. Braga Kul, before the end, reads Eric cold:

Braga Kul: "We've been here for five months. This is the first time any of you have even found us." Braga Kul: "You pretend like you're so holy... you stink of aberration. I know what you are."

At [5:05] he drops to his knees and surrenders. He is knocked out, bound, and gagged — alive, captive, un-interrogated.

10. The diamond [5:05–5:08]

A potion won't fix a cut throat. Eric opens the top of Dario's skull with a dagger and casts Revivify through a diamond; it grows tenfold and fills the cavity, the wound scabbing dark.

DM (Dave): "You, like the Empress, now have a diamond head... twinning."

While dead, Dario saw something: "I saw the dragon... dragons... I saw it here... white sands." Lingering injury (16): an eye, permanently — everything he attacks now effectively has light cover. He comes back at 1 HP, one horn short, with a new scar.

11. Clearing, burning, camping [5:08–5:28]

The Mountain Door is declared cleared. Loot and the campaign book (see Intel). Prisoners Pal and Audrey — Korranberg scavengers — are freed by a fox-masked Gemma and offer a meal in town anytime. Gemma then burns every corpse in the complex — faces destroyed first, eleven flasks of alchemist's fire, everything into the chasm — including the five bodies from the bags of holding (the real Deirdre, Graham, Sir Chalupa, the fortune teller, and the dog), explicitly so Uriel can never raise them. The Grand Stair down is blocked with large rocks and dirt; the DM seeds that the forge itself may not be in this front structure at all — the rail entered the mountain elsewhere. Long rest, captive under guard.

Intel & open threads

  • The campaign book: ~20-strong Urukoth recon regiment, emplaced months (Braga claims five), observing troop movements in and out of Korth. The war is getting closer to Korth; the first troops are moving. They fought "things that came up from deeper inside" and blocked the way down rather than fight on.
  • Braga Kul is alive and un-interrogated. The question asked at the table and never answered: are the orcs here for the same reason we are? He also smelled the LeBeefe taint on Eric — he knows something worth asking about.
  • The accident is dated: ~3 years ago, same year as Sharn's fall — and the Trust's own tally says ~97% of the dwarvish workforce died.
  • Deirdre's clearance includes the Lazaret. Nobody said the quiet part aloud, but the endgame infiltration path may already be in Gemma's coat pocket.
  • Dario's death-vision: dragons, "I saw it here," white sands. (The module knows what lives under the mountain.)
  • The descent is blocked — rocks and dirt at the Grand Stair; alternatives are the deeper caves or finding the rail mouth.
  • Party strategy declared [0:52]: pre-position Silver Flame units near the 13 Master-bell sites so any silencing method can be executed everywhere at once.
